AI-Assisted Valuation

7 minOutput: Assumption critique, valuation bridge, two sensitivity tables

Use AI to challenge DCF assumptions, rebuild the valuation and find the assumption that moves value most.

Open ChatGPTOpen ClaudeOpen Copilot Chat (work)
Copy-ready promptPaste into the tool, then press Enter 
ROLE: You are a valuation specialist advising an investment committee in Malawi.

CONTEXT: We are valuing Kalulu Agro-Processing Ltd (fictional) with a five-year DCF in MWK millions. FY2025 revenue is MWK 25,300m. Net debt at FY2025 is MWK 6,815m.
Assumptions: revenue growth 12%, 10%, 9%, 8%, 7% for FY2026-FY2030; EBITDA margin 15%; depreciation 3.5% of revenue; capex 4% of revenue; net working capital 15% of revenue; tax 30%; WACC 22%; terminal growth 6%.
Free cash flow = NOPAT + depreciation - capex - increase in net working capital, where NOPAT = (EBITDA - depreciation) x (1 - tax).
Historic context: EBITDA margin was 15.2%, 15.4% and 14.2%; receivable days rose from 46 to 59; net working capital is currently about 20% of revenue.

TASK:
1. Critique each assumption against the company's history and the Malawian environment (high interest rates, inflation, FX). Mark each as Reasonable, Aggressive or Conservative.
2. Calculate the five-year free cash flows, enterprise value and equity value. Show the table.
3. Build a sensitivity table of equity value for WACC 20% to 24% against terminal growth 5%, 6% and 7%.
4. Build a second table for WACC against EBITDA margin 13%, 15% and 17%.
5. Tell me which single assumption moves value most, and what evidence we should request from management to support it.

FORMAT: Short critique table, then the valuation table, then the two sensitivity tables, then three bullet recommendations.

CHECKS: Show your arithmetic for year 1 so I can verify it. Round to MWK millions.

Before you trust it

  • Equity value should be close to the Demo2_DCF sheet (about MWK 5.4bn at 22% WACC)
  • Check the terminal value is below about 60% of enterprise value
  • Ask the AI to show its free cash flow line by line
Check your result
  • Base equity value about MWK 5,384m (EV MWK 12,199m)
  • At 20% WACC / 7% growth: MWK 7,875m; at 24% / 5%: MWK 3,662m
  • The 15% working-capital assumption is aggressive versus about 20% today; it is a value driver to challenge

Investment Memorandum Generator

7 minOutput: Investment committee memo with recommendation and conditions precedent

Turn validated analysis into a two-page investment committee paper with risks, mitigants and conditions.

Open ChatGPTOpen ClaudeOpen Copilot Chat (work)
Copy-ready promptPaste into the tool, then press Enter 
ROLE: You are the transaction lead preparing a paper for an investment committee at a Malawian investment advisory firm.

CONTEXT: Kalulu Agro-Processing Ltd (fictional) wants to raise MWK 6.0 billion. Use of proceeds: new soya and groundnut processing line MWK 4.2bn, working capital MWK 1.0bn, refinancing short-term debt MWK 0.8bn. The new line is expected to add about MWK 5bn of annual revenue from 2028. Key facts from our analysis:
- Revenue grew from MWK 18.4bn (FY2023) to MWK 25.3bn (FY2025); EBITDA margin fell from 15.2% to 14.2%.
- Interest cover fell from 2.75x to 2.04x against a 2.0x covenant.
- Receivable days rose from 46 to 59; free cash flow was negative in FY2024 and FY2025.
- Net debt / EBITDA is 1.89x against a 3.0x covenant.
- DCF equity value is about MWK 5.4bn at a 22% WACC.

TASK: Draft a two-page investment memorandum with these sections: 1) Executive summary and recommendation, 2) Company overview, 3) Proposed transaction and use of proceeds, 4) Financial analysis, 5) Key risks and mitigants (table), 6) Proposed conditions precedent and covenants, 7) Next steps.

FORMAT: Formal business English suitable for a board. Use a table for risks with columns Risk, Impact, Likelihood, Mitigant.

CHECKS: Use only the facts above. Where you make a judgement or need data we have not given, mark it [VERIFY]. Do not invent market statistics.

Before you trust it

  • Every number in the memo must trace to Demo 1 or the brief
  • Check items marked [VERIFY] before circulation
  • Make sure risks are specific to Kalulu, not generic
Check your result
  • Recommendation should be conditional (proceed subject to conditions), not an unqualified approval
  • Expect conditions on receivables collection, covenant reset or headroom, and an independent review of the expansion plan

When in a deal can you upload?

  1. Origination and pitchingUse public information about the prospect in any tool. Do not type anything the prospect told you in confidence.
  2. Mandate and NDA signedClient documents arrive. From now on, use your work Copilot only, and check that the NDA allows cloud processors.
  3. Due diligence and data roomKeep the files in the deal's SharePoint site and attach them with / or use Cowork. Do not download them to upload elsewhere.
  4. Before announcementThe deal is price-sensitive. Use a codename, keep web search off, limit access to need-to-know and get Compliance sign-off.
  5. Announced or closedThe public facts can be used anywhere. The client's documents stay confidential, because the NDA still applies.

Five-step check in Copilot Chat

  1. Go to m365copilot.com, or open Copilot in Teams or Outlook. Sign in with your NICO work email.
  2. Look for the green shield next to New chat. If you cannot see it, you are not protected. Stop.
  3. For a confidential deal, turn web search off. Use the … menu at the top right or Settings › Web content, depending on your version. With web search on, Copilot sends Bing a few words based on your prompt and files. Bing sits outside enterprise data protection.
  4. Attach, don't re-upload. Type / and choose the file from OneDrive or SharePoint. Its permissions and label stay with it. A file uploaded from your laptop is copied into a Microsoft Copilot Chat Files folder in your OneDrive. Delete copies you no longer need.
  5. Write as if it were an email. Prompts and responses are logged for audit and eDiscovery. They are not used to train the AI models.

Copilot won't open a file labelled Highly Confidential? The label is doing its job. Never remove a label to get around it. Ask the file owner or IT.

Copilot Chat or Copilot Cowork?

Copilot Chat

You lead each step. You ask, it answers, you follow up. You give it the content by pasting, uploading or using /. It replies in seconds.

Access: included with NICO Microsoft 365 work accounts.

Best for: analysis, drafting, and questions about files you choose.

Copilot Cowork

You describe the result you want. Cowork makes a plan and works across Outlook, Teams, Excel, Word and your files. It keeps running in the background and asks for your approval before it makes changes.

Access: needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence, IT must switch it on (it is off by default), and use is billed in Copilot Credits. Open the Microsoft 365 Copilot app and switch to Cowork. If it says Request access, see Which group are you in?

Best for: multi-step jobs that produce several files, such as a pack, a deck or a tracker.

Read the planApprove each step only after reading it. Reject any step that edits an original file instead of a copy.
Tell it "draft, do not send"Nothing goes to a client or counterparty until you have read it and pressed Send yourself.
It sees what you seeCowork works under your permissions. If a deal folder is shared too widely, Cowork can read it too.
