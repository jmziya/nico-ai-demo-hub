AI-Assisted Valuation

7 minOutput: Assumption critique, valuation bridge, two sensitivity tables

Use AI to challenge DCF assumptions, rebuild the valuation and find the assumption that moves value most.

Open ChatGPTOpen ClaudeOpen Copilot Chat (work)
Copy-ready promptPaste into the tool, then press Enter 
ROLE: You are a valuation specialist advising an investment committee in Malawi.

CONTEXT: We are valuing Kalulu Agro-Processing Ltd (fictional) with a five-year DCF in MWK millions. FY2025 revenue is MWK 25,300m. Net debt at FY2025 is MWK 6,815m.
Assumptions: revenue growth 12%, 10%, 9%, 8%, 7% for FY2026-FY2030; EBITDA margin 15%; depreciation 3.5% of revenue; capex 4% of revenue; net working capital 15% of revenue; tax 30%; WACC 22%; terminal growth 6%.
Free cash flow = NOPAT + depreciation - capex - increase in net working capital, where NOPAT = (EBITDA - depreciation) x (1 - tax).
Historic context: EBITDA margin was 15.2%, 15.4% and 14.2%; receivable days rose from 46 to 59; net working capital is currently about 20% of revenue.

TASK:
1. Critique each assumption against the company's history and the Malawian environment (high interest rates, inflation, FX). Mark each as Reasonable, Aggressive or Conservative.
2. Calculate the five-year free cash flows, enterprise value and equity value. Show the table.
3. Build a sensitivity table of equity value for WACC 20% to 24% against terminal growth 5%, 6% and 7%.
4. Build a second table for WACC against EBITDA margin 13%, 15% and 17%.
5. Tell me which single assumption moves value most, and what evidence we should request from management to support it.

FORMAT: Short critique table, then the valuation table, then the two sensitivity tables, then three bullet recommendations.

CHECKS: Show your arithmetic for year 1 so I can verify it. Round to MWK millions.

Before you trust it

  • Equity value should be close to the Demo2_DCF sheet (about MWK 5.4bn at 22% WACC)
  • Check the terminal value is below about 60% of enterprise value
  • Ask the AI to show its free cash flow line by line
Check your result
  • Base equity value about MWK 5,384m (EV MWK 12,199m)
  • At 20% WACC / 7% growth: MWK 7,875m; at 24% / 5%: MWK 3,662m
  • The 15% working-capital assumption is aggressive versus about 20% today; it is a value driver to challenge

Year-1 revenue under a power purchase agreement MWK 12,000m escalating 5% a year; year-1 opex MWK 1,800m escalating 7%. 20-year life, tax 30%. The offtaker is the national electricity utility. Model results:

Scenario | Project IRR | Equity IRR | Min DSCR | Avg DSCR

Revenue -15% (tariff/curtailment) | 15.8% | 18.9% | 1.15x | 1.32x

Combined downside | 13.3% | 10.3% | 0.83x | 1.0x

1. Interpret the results for a lender and for an equity investor. Which scenarios are financeable if lenders require a minimum DSCR of 1.25x?
2. Build a risk allocation matrix covering at least eight risks (construction, completion, offtaker payment, foreign exchange, tariff and regulatory approval, resource/irradiation, operations, interest rate, political/force majeure). Columns: Risk, Description, Allocated to, Mitigation instrument.

FORMAT: Numbered sections with tables where useful.

CHECKS: Do not change the model numbers. If you suggest new figures, label them as assumptions.

Before you trust it

  • Re-run one scenario in the Demo4_ProjectFinance sheet by changing the blue inputs
  • Check the AI does not treat DSCR below 1.0x as acceptable
  • Confirm Malawi-specific risks appear (offtaker, FX, tariff approval)
Check your result
  • Base min DSCR 1.34x passes 1.25x; revenue -15% (1.15x), capex +20% (1.16x) and rate +4 pts (1.2x) fail
  • Combined downside min DSCR 0.83x: cash cannot cover debt service

Capital Raising Pitchbook

7 minOutput: Options comparison, recommendation, indicative term sheet, pitchbook outline

Compare three ways to raise MWK 6bn, recommend one, then draft a term sheet and a ten-slide investor pitchbook outline.

Open ChatGPTOpen ClaudeOpen Copilot Chat (work)
Copy-ready promptPaste into the tool, then press Enter 
ROLE: You are a debt capital markets adviser at a Malawian corporate finance firm.

CONTEXT: Kalulu Agro-Processing Ltd (fictional) needs MWK 6.0bn (processing line MWK 4.2bn, working capital MWK 1.0bn, refinancing MWK 0.8bn). FY2025: revenue MWK 25.3bn, EBITDA MWK 3.6bn, interest cover 2.04x, net debt/EBITDA 1.89x. Three indicative options:

Option: 5-year senior unsecured corporate bond (listed on MSE)
Amount (MWK m): 6,000
Indicative cost: 23.0% fixed, semi-annual coupon
Tenor: 5 years, bullet
Security: Unsecured; negative pledge
Key covenants: Net debt/EBITDA <= 3.0x; interest cover >= 2.0x
Pros: Locks in fixed rate; broadens investor base (pension funds, insurers)
Cons: Arranging, listing and trustee costs; public disclosure

Option: Bank term loan (syndicated)
Amount (MWK m): 6,000
Indicative cost: Reference rate + 3% (approx. 26% floating)
Tenor: 5 years, amortising
Security: Fixed and floating charge over assets
Key covenants: DSCR >= 1.3x; gearing <= 2.0x
Pros: Faster to arrange; flexible drawdown
Cons: Floating rate exposure; heavy security; amortisation strains cash

Option: Rights issue + private equity placement
Amount (MWK m): 6,000
Indicative cost: Cost of equity approx. 28-30%
Tenor: Permanent
Security: None
Key covenants: Shareholder agreement; board seat for investor
Pros: Reduces gearing; no fixed repayment
Cons: Dilution; most expensive capital; valuation negotiation

TASK:
1. Compare the three options on cost, risk to the company, speed, covenant burden and investor appetite. Recommend one option or a combination, with reasons.
2. Draft an indicative term sheet for a 5-year listed corporate bond: issuer, amount, tenor, coupon, payment frequency, use of proceeds, security, covenants, listing, trustee, arranger, target investors.
3. Outline a 10-slide investor pitchbook: slide title and the one key message per slide.
4. Write a 120-word investor narrative.

FORMAT: Comparison table, recommendation paragraph, term sheet as a two-column table, pitchbook as a numbered list.

CHECKS: Mark all terms as indicative and subject to market testing and regulatory approval. Do not quote real market rates.

Before you trust it

  • Term sheet terms must match the options data, not invented market rates
  • Check covenants are realistic given 2.04x interest cover
  • Have compliance review any investor-facing wording
Check your result
  • A sound answer explains why a bond's fixed rate helps with interest cover so close to 2.0x
  • A combination (smaller bond plus equity) is a defensible recommendation if it reduces gearing
