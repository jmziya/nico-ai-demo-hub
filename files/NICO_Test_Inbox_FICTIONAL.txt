NICO CAPITAL AI MASTERCLASS - TEST INBOX (FICTIONAL - TRAINING USE ONLY)

Ten fictional emails for the Email Agent 2.0 inbox challenge.
How to use: send each one to yourself from Outlook (or the facilitator sends all ten to every participant before the session). Copy the Subject line exactly, including [TRAINING], and paste the Body. The "Sender" line goes at the top of the body, because the email will arrive from your own address.
All people, companies and figures are fictional.
