Kalulu Agro-Processing Ltd - EXTRACT FROM MINUTES OF THE BOARD MEETING HELD 14 MAY 2026 (FICTIONAL)

Item 4 Tax matters: The Finance Director reported that the revenue authority has issued an assessment of MWK 420 million relating to disputed VAT claims for FY2022-FY2023. The company has lodged an objection. External advisers estimate a 50% probability of an adverse outcome. The board resolved to monitor the matter.
Item 5 Customers: The board noted that Southern Commodities Export Ltd accounted for approximately 17% of FY2025 sales and that collections from this customer have slowed significantly.
Item 6 Banking: The Finance Director advised that the lender has been informed informally that the interest cover covenant may be under pressure for FY2025. A waiver request will be considered if needed.
Item 7 Procurement: The board approved continued purchases from Mapira Farms Ltd without a competitive tender, given reliability of supply.
